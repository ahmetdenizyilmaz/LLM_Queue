Keyboard Shortcuts & Privacy

Fast controls and transparent local-only storage guarantees.

Shortcuts

Toggle LLM Queue panel Alt + Q
Pause or resume queue Alt + Shift + P
Queue text directly from chat input Ctrl / Cmd + Shift + Enter
Queue automatically while AI is busy Enter

You can customize global extension shortcuts anytime at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

100% Local & Private

LLM Queue runs completely inside your browser. Your prompts, queues, presets, and settings are stored exclusively in local browser storage (chrome.storage.local). No data is ever transmitted to external servers, analytics, or telemetry endpoints.
